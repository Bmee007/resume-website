# 3D model credits

Each model listed in manifest.json, with its source and licence.
- **computer** (`computer.glb`) — desktop workstation (thin-bezel monitor on stand, TKL keyboard, ergonomic mouse). Procedurally generated geometry (three.js in Node, no external assets), author: Borina Keo, licence: CC0 / own work.
- **brain** — `brain.glb`. Source: NIH 3D entry 3DPX-020960 "Brain, Male" (https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-020960), HRA 3D Reference Organ for Brain, Male v1.3 by Kristen Browne & Heidi Schlehlein (Human Reference Atlas / NIAID), built from Allen Human Brain Atlas data (Ding et al. 2016); DOI 10.48539/HBM929.XKCL.339. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Modified: internal structures removed, meshes joined, simplified to ~129k tris, meshopt-compressed.

- **warehouse** (`warehouse.glb`) — distribution centre / logistics warehouse. Procedurally generated geometry (three.js BoxGeometry/CylinderGeometry/ExtrudeGeometry in Node, merged and meshopt-compressed with glTF-Transform). Author: generated for this site; no third-party assets. Licence: CC0 / public domain.
- **truck** (`truck.glb`) — North American sleeper tractor + 53' dry van. Geometry generated procedurally for this site with three.js (lofts/lathes/extrusions; no third-party asset), compressed with glTF-Transform (EXT_meshopt_compression). Author: Borina Keo (own work). Licence: CC0 1.0.
- **canada** (`canada.glb`) — physical relief model of Canada (cut-slab style, real coastline, lakes, DEM relief, shallow province grooves). Outline/lakes/boundaries: Natural Earth 10m `admin_0_countries_lakes`, `admin_1_states_provinces_lines`, `lakes` (https://www.naturalearthdata.com, public domain). Elevation: ETOPO1 Global Relief Model, Amante & Eakins 2009, NOAA NCEI, doi:10.7289/V5C8276M (public domain, U.S. Government work), fetched as a 2-arc-minute subset via NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP `etopo180`. Geometry generated for this site (Lambert conformal conic 49°/77°/−96° matching `src/scripts/shapes.js`, mainland bbox fitted to x ∈ [−1.4, 1.4], north = −z, sea level at y = 0, slab 0.05 thick; relief h = 0.0366·(elev km)^0.75 units ≈ 40–60× vertical exaggeration), simplified and meshopt-compressed with glTF-Transform. Author: Borina Keo. Licence: CC0 / public domain.
- **pills** (`pills.glb`) — North-American amber prescription vial with child-resistant push-and-turn cap, wrapped pharmacy label with raised Rx symbol, loose capsules and scored tablets. Procedurally generated geometry for this site (three.js lathes/parametric surfaces/extrusions in Node, no third-party asset; `tools/models/build-pills.mjs`), compressed with glTF-Transform (EXT_meshopt_compression). Author: Borina Keo (own work). Licence: CC0 1.0.
- **ledger** (`ledger.glb`) — open A5 hardcover ledger with ribbon bookmark and posted fountain pen. Procedurally generated geometry (three.js in Node: lofted page-layer rings, displaced top-page grid with embossed ledger rulings, lathed pen with extruded nib, parallel-transport ribbon sweep; no third-party assets; `tools/models/build-ledger.mjs`), meshopt-compressed with glTF-Transform. Author: Borina Keo (own work). Licence: CC0 1.0.
- **bot** (`bot.glb`) — "Illumi", seated companion/assistant robot (visor head, articulated neck, egg torso with panel seams and chest ring, domed shoulders, three-finger hands, waist ring, rounded base). Procedurally generated geometry (three.js in Node: displacement-mapped lathes/ellipsoids meshed with an adaptive quadtree; no third-party assets; `tools/models/build-bot.mjs`), simplified and meshopt-compressed with glTF-Transform. Author: Borina Keo (own work). Licence: CC0 1.0.
